Battery SOS

MacBook battery warning app

Battery SOS lives in the menu bar. The free warning is Default Warning: a full-screen alert you can set from 1% to 10%. It starts at 1%. Connect power, press Escape, or use the close button to dismiss it.

Free

  • Default Warning
  • Warning percentage from 1% to 10%, default 1%
  • Mute sound effects, start at login, and Test SOS

Pro, $1 one time

  • Vitals Monitor, Self-Destruct, Reactor Meltdown, Starship Life Support, and Matrix
  • Battery dashboard
  • Early heads-up at 15%, 20%, 30%, or 40%
  • Snooze for 5, 10, or 15 minutes while on battery above 1%
  • Charge reminders at 80%, 90%, or 100%. Reminders only. They do not control charging.
  • Quiet hours for sound only

How much does Battery SOS cost?

The download includes Default Warning for free. Pro is $1 (1.00 USD), one time. Pro adds Vitals Monitor, Self-Destruct, Reactor Meltdown, Starship Life Support, Matrix, the battery dashboard, early heads-up at 15%, 20%, 30%, or 40%, snooze, charge reminders, and quiet hours. Charge reminders do not control charging.

Does Battery SOS work on Intel Macs?

No. This build is Apple silicon only and requires macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
