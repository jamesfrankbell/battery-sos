Battery SOS

Terms

Battery SOS is a menu-bar app for Apple silicon Macs on macOS 13 or later. These terms describe the product that ships in this repository. Items that still need a decision are marked TODO and are not promises.

Free and Pro

Default Warning is free. The full-screen warning can be set from 1% to 10% and starts at 1%. You can mute sound, start the installed app at login, and preview it with Test SOS.

Pro is a one-time $1 purchase of a license key. It unlocks Vitals Monitor, Self-Destruct, Reactor Meltdown, Starship Life Support, and Matrix, plus the battery dashboard, early heads-up at 15%, 20%, 30%, or 40%, snooze for 5, 10, or 15 minutes, charge reminders at 80%, 90%, or 100%, and quiet hours for sound only.

Charge reminders do not control charging. They do not start, stop, or limit it. Quiet hours do not hide the full-screen warning. Snooze is unavailable at 1% and while the Mac is on power.

License

Checkout is Stripe Checkout at pay.batterysos.app. The price charged is the Stripe price configured for this product. The published price is $1, one time. A key activates on one Mac. Enter it in Settings, then Enter License Key. Self-service recovery by email is not available.

Refund policy

Pro is a one-time license purchase, not a subscription. Paying does not by itself renew. A refund, if one is issued, is a return of that one-time charge through Stripe.

The app

Battery SOS reads the battery level macOS reports and shows a warning. It does not replace the Mac's own power management. Time-remaining figures on the Pro dashboard come from macOS and can change.
