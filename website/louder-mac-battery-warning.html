Battery SOS

A louder MacBook battery warning

Battery SOS does not raise the volume of the macOS low-battery chime. Default Warning covers the screen and plays its own sound until you plug in or dismiss it. The sound is part of the app. Mute Sound Effects turns it off.

How do I get a louder battery alert on a MacBook?

Battery SOS does not turn up the macOS alert volume. Default Warning fills the screen and plays its own sound until you connect power or dismiss it. Mute Sound Effects turns that sound off. Pro quiet hours silence the sound only, and the full-screen warning still appears.

The free threshold is 1% to 10%, and it starts at 1%. Quiet hours are off until you enable them. Their default window is 22:00–07:00. They do not hide the screen.

Does Battery SOS work on Intel Macs?

No. This build is Apple silicon only and requires macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.

Pro is $1, one time, if you want the other themes, the dashboard, an earlier heads-up, snooze, charge reminders, or quiet hours. Charge reminders do not control charging.
