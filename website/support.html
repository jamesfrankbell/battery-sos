Buy Pro

Pro is $1, one time. Use Buy Pro on the Battery SOS site, or in the app choose Settings, then Unlock Pro ($1).... Checkout runs at pay.batterysos.app and opens Stripe. The success page shows the license key.

Enter the key

In Battery SOS, choose Settings, then Enter License Key. One key activates on one Mac. The free warning percentage stays available from 1% to 10%, default 1%, without a key.
